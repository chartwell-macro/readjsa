binary installations carry the pinned upstream notice and permission text.

`0df6b8b14adafc47bd12f1b19effb217fb0ef236bda41a7dedd40d145362ecad`.

`read_salm()` and adds official release discovery, exact provenance, offline
contracts, semantic spreadsheet parsing and strict schema validation.
